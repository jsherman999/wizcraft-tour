Wizcraft is a strategy game played on a real Minecraft world, from your web browser. Everyone on your home network joins from a browser, takes a seat at the war table and leads a side.

Instead of steering one character, you lead units — scouts, builders, miners, fighters, archers, generals and a wizard — who walk, dig, build and fight in the Minecraft world while you give orders from a map. You can also drop into any one of them and play it yourself.
